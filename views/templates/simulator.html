Models channel decoherence via Qiskit Aer's depolarizing_error ($\mathcal{E}(\rho) = (1-\epsilon)\rho + \frac{\epsilon}{3}(X\rho X + Y\rho Y + Z\rho Z)$). Noise rates exceeding $11.00\%$ force the asymptotic secret key rate to drop to zero.

1. Single-Photon State Preparation

Alice generates random classical bits $b_i \in \{0, 1\}$ and assigns each to rectilinear ($+$: $|0\rangle, |1\rangle$) or diagonal ($\times$: $|+\rangle, |-\rangle$) polarizations.

2. Quantum No-Cloning Theorem

Wootters & Zurek (1982) proved that an unknown quantum state cannot be copied without disturbance. Any measurement by Eve introduces irreversible entropy.

3. Basis Sifting & Estimation

Bob projects incoming qubits onto independent random bases. Over an authenticated classical channel, they compare bases and discard mismatches ($\approx 50\%$).

4. Devetak-Winter Asymptotic Secret Key Rate

If $\text{QBER} < 11.00\%$, one-way error correction and privacy amplification guarantee a distillable secret key rate $R_\infty \ge \max(0, 1 - 2h(\text{QBER}))$.
